Each word is timed separately. Use / to split a word into syllables (Hel/lo) or \/ for a literal slash. Blank lines are ignored. Editing lyrics keeps timings of lines that did not change.

ms

While the song plays: press Space as each word starts. Optionally press F or J when the word just sung ends; do this before a pause. A word with no end lasts until the next word starts. Ends cannot be set after a syllable (Hel/lo), only after a whole word.

Z or Backspace undoes the last press. Enter plays or pauses. Home returns to the start. Left/Right nudge the selected time by 10 ms (Shift: 100 ms). Double-click a word to continue tapping from it. Drag markers on the waveform to adjust them.

The Seek slider jumps anywhere in the track. Focus it, then use Left/Right (10 ms; Shift: 5 s), Page Up/Page Down, Home or End. Clicking the waveform also seeks.
